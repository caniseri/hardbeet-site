Delete your account

You can delete your HardBeet account in the app or request deletion here if you no longer have app access.

In the app

Open HardBeet and go to Settings > Privacy and account > Delete account.

Request deletion by email

Send a deletion request from the email account you use for HardBeet when possible. If you use phone, Apple, or Google sign-in, include that sign-in method and the email address or phone number associated with the account so support can verify the request. Never send a password or sign-in code.

Email deletion request Read privacy policy

What deletion covers

Account deletion ends sharing and removes the account profile, settings, legal-acceptance records, connections, daily check-ins, invites, and push notification token records and associated personal data, unless a limited record must be retained for law, security, fraud prevention, or a legal claim. Provider backups may retain deleted information for a limited backup cycle before being overwritten.

The Privacy Policy describes retention and your privacy rights. If retention applies, HardBeet will explain it during the deletion support flow.
