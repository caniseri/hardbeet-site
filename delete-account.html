Delete your account

You can delete your HardBeet account in the app or request deletion here if you no longer have app access.

In the app

Open HardBeet and go to Settings > Privacy and account > Delete account.

Request deletion by email

Send a deletion request from the email account you use for HardBeet when possible. If you use phone, Apple, or Google sign-in, include that sign-in method and the email address or phone number associated with the account so support can verify the request.

Email deletion request Read privacy policy

What deletion covers

Account deletion removes the HardBeet account record and associated personal data that HardBeet is not legally or operationally required to retain, including profile, daily check-in, invite, approved-connection, and push notification token records.

Some records may be retained for troubleshooting, security, legal, or abuse-prevention reasons. If retention applies, HardBeet will explain it during the deletion support flow.
