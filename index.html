How it works

A calm routine, not a monitor.

HardBeet keeps a daily touchpoint between independent adults and the people who care about them — on clear, agreed terms.

1

Check in with one tap

Tap “I'm here today” once a day, at a daily time you choose. That tap is the only thing that creates a check-in.

2

Invite people you approve

Share an invite code with someone you trust. They review and accept a plain-language agreement before they see anything.

3

They see today's signal

Approved loved ones see whether today's check-in happened — shown in your time zone and theirs. Nothing beyond the agreement is shared.

When a day slips

Gentle first. Always.

Life happens — travel, rest, a phone left in another room. HardBeet is built to stay calm about it.

You hear first

If your daily time passes without a check-in, HardBeet reminds you before anyone else is told.

Followers hear one hour later

If there's still no check-in an hour after your daily time, approved loved ones get one clear notification — with a gentle suggestion, never an alarm.

Pause when you mean to be quiet

Traveling, resting, or intentionally offline? Pause sharing for 24 hours, 7 days, or until you turn it back on. No missed check-in notifications are sent while paused.

Private by design

They see a small daily signal. Not your life.

No surveillance, no live map, no activity feed. The agreement is plain about what's shared — and what never is.

What HardBeet is not

HardBeet is not an emergency response service, a medical monitor, or proof that someone is safe. Notifications are best effort and depend on phones and networks. If someone may need urgent help, contact local services or another real-world support channel directly.

FAQ

Fair questions.

Does HardBeet share my location?

No. HardBeet has no live location feature at all. Approved loved ones see the agreed check-in, timing, and pause details described above. They never see live location or private phone activity.

What happens if I miss a check-in?

You get a reminder first. If there's still no check-in one hour after your daily time, your approved loved ones receive a single notification suggesting a call or text may help. Notifications are best effort and should never replace real-world contact.

Who can see my check-in?

Only people you invite and approve. Each connection starts with a plain-language agreement, and you can pause or stop sharing at any time from the app.

Can followers quiet notifications overnight?

Yes. Followers can quiet overnight notifications or hold them until morning, so a late check-in never turns into a 3 AM wake-up.

How do I delete my account?

From Settings → Privacy and account → Delete account, or by following the steps on our account deletion page. Deletion removes your account and associated personal data.
